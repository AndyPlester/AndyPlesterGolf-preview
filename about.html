The expert behind the system

Complete-game coaching, with a short-game specialism

PGA Professional | Master Club Fitter | TaylorMade Ambassador

I'm Andy Plester, a PGA Professional with 23 years of coaching experience across the UK, Ireland and New Zealand, and the author of The Up & Down Game. I coach in person at Ullesthorpe Court Hotel & Golf Club in Leicestershire, and online worldwide through Skillest.

With over 20 years of club-fitting experience, I combine technical equipment knowledge with expert coaching to help golfers improve their performance and find equipment suited to their individual game.

I coach the complete game: full swing and ball striking, driving and iron play, short game and putting, course management, practice planning, performance and confidence. The short game is a particular specialism, but every golfer receives coaching built around their own needs.

No gimmicks. No quick fixes. Just structured skill development and a clear pathway you can actually follow.

Whether you are a beginner, an improving club golfer or a competitive player, my job is to make your next step clear and give you purposeful work that transfers to the course.

Why I built the system

From random practice to a clear pathway

Over the years I noticed the same thing again and again: talented golfers practicing hard, but with no system for the short game. They'd hit hundreds of balls around the practice green and never once rehearse the shot that was costing them.

The Up & Down System is my specialist short-game framework: the CLEAR Process for reading every shot, the Four Stages that build scoring skill in order, and practice games that make it stick under pressure. It sits alongside my wider coaching for the complete game.
