How does the free golf assessment work?

Open the assessment on Skillest and submit a video of the area of your game you want Andy to review. Andy will assess it and send personalised feedback through Skillest.

You will receive personalised feedback to help you understand what may be holding your golf back and what to work on next. The assessment can cover full swing, short game or putting.

Yes. The initial golf assessment is free, and you do not need to subscribe to ongoing coaching to receive it.

You can use Andy’s feedback as your next step. If you would like more support, you may choose to explore Andy’s coaching options, but there is no obligation.
