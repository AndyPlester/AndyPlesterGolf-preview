The Four Stages

Every shot, drill and lesson maps to four stages

Get each stage working and your short game becomes more consistent, more predictable, and more reliable when it matters most.

1

Control The Strike

Setup · Low point · Tempo

Can you control where the club meets the ground? Build a clean, repeatable strike.

2

Control The Shot

Distance · Trajectory · Spin

Can you control what the ball does? Control the flight, distance and rollout.

3

Control The Decision

Read · Choose · Commit

Can you pick the right shot? Assess the situation and choose the best option.

4

Control The Performance

Pressure · Focus · Commitment

Can you do it under pressure? Perform your best when it matters most.

Questions about starting

Free assessment questions

Everything you need to take the first step with confidence.

What happens in the free golf assessment?

You send Andy videos of your golf swing through Skillest. Andy reviews them personally and gives you clear feedback on what to work on first.

Do I need special equipment for online golf coaching?

No. You only need your phone and somewhere safe to film your swing; your lessons and feedback are delivered through Skillest.

Can Andy coach my whole golf game online?

Yes. Andy provides complete personalised golf coaching, with particular expertise in the short game and scoring.

How do I continue after the free assessment?

Andy will explain the most useful next step for your game. If ongoing support is right for you, Performance Coaching includes regular lessons, analysis and a personalised practice plan through Skillest.
