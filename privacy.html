Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Andy Plester Golf (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website or services.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as when you:

  • Request a golf assessment
  • Contact us by email or through our website
  • Subscribe to updates or newsletters

This may include your name, email address, and any message or preferences you submit.

How we use your information

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide the services you request
  • Send you relevant updates, tips, or marketing (where you have agreed)
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal or regulatory requirements

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only where necessary with service providers who assist us (e.g. email or hosting), or if required by law.

Cookies and similar technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve your experience (e.g. remembering preferences or analysing site use). You can adjust your browser settings to limit or block cookies.

Website analytics

Website analytics are not currently active. We will update this policy before adding analytics or other visitor tracking.

Data retention and security

We keep your data only for as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorised access or loss.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights or ask questions, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised when we do.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact us at andrewplesterpga@gmail.com.
