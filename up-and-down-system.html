A smarter approach to the short game. Discover the four stages that help golfers improve their strike, control their shots, make better decisions and perform under pressure.

The Four Stages

Build your short game in the right order

Each stage builds on the last, taking you from reliable contact to confident performance on the course.

1

Foundation

Control the Strike

Control where the club meets the ground and build clean, repeatable contact.

2

Skill

Control the Shot

Develop trajectory, distance and spin so you can play the shot the situation requires.

3

Strategy

Control the Decision

Read the lie, choose the right shot and commit to a clear plan before you play.

4

Scoring

Control the Performance

Use purposeful practice and pressure games to take your skills onto the course.

Short-Game Coaching Questions

Build a Short Game You Can Trust

What does short-game golf coaching cover?

Andy’s short-game coaching covers strike, trajectory, distance, spin, shot choice and performing under pressure. The aim is to help you make better decisions and get up and down more often.

Can I improve my short game through online coaching?

Yes. You can send videos of your chipping, pitching or bunker play through Skillest, then receive personalised analysis and clear practice priorities from Andy.

What is The Up & Down System?

The Up & Down System is Andy Plester’s four-stage framework for building a dependable short game. It develops strike, shot control, decision-making and performance in that order.
